1 / 6
Rule 1 • Saturdays 3:00 AM – 11:00 PM

When is attendance open?

Attendance opens every Saturday from 3:00 AM to 11:00 PM Nepal Time. Choose Present or Absent directly from your phone.

Rule 2 • On-Time & Continuous Streak 9:50 AM Cutoff

Continuous On-Time Streak & Score

Submit before or at 9:50 AM to earn an On-Time point. When you submit on time Saturday after Saturday continuously without a single gap, you grow your 🔥 On-Time Streak! If you miss or submit late, your streak resets, but your monthly On-Time total is always saved and shown in the table.

Rule 3 • Free Holiday 0 Points

1 Free Holiday each month

Every month, your first absent or late submission costs 0 penalty points! It simply uses your 1 monthly allowed holiday so you are never penalized for occasional emergencies.

Rule 4 • Penalty Points +1 Point each

Watch out for penalty points!

If you've already used your free holiday for the month, any subsequent late or absent attendance adds +1 penalty point. Accumulating 10 points leads to an official choir Fine.

Rule 5 • Reward Bonus -1 Point applied

Earn a -1 Point bonus reward!

If you attend on time for all working Saturdays in a month (e.g. 4 on-time attendances in a 4-Saturday month), you earn an automatic -1 point deduction from your total points!

Rule 6 • Saturday 11:00 PM Auto-assigned

Forgot to fill the form?

At 11:00 PM Saturday, missing forms are automatically recorded on the rule basis. If you still have your free holiday, it uses it (0 pts); if already used, it adds 1 point. Always submit during the day!
